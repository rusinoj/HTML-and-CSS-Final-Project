Baus's Top Champion Picks

Sion

  • Baus's #1 pick.
  • Infamous unique playstyle on Sion:
  • Getting ahead while dying very often.
  • Sion can fight after death and gets bonuses on all kills.
  • Full offense or defense, will scale into late game either way if played aggressively enough.

Quinn and Valor

  • Best map-presence pick.
  • Fly to quickly catch waves, fights, or recall to buy items.
  • High damage and mobility ensures 1v1 potential.
  • Can also fly away - always in enemy's face.

Gragas

  • Favorite AP alternate to Sion.
  • Has sustain, clear, and scirmish tools.
  • Constantly drunk (funny design).
  • Great combos, mobility, and tools for applying pressure.
  • Lots of AOE(Area of Effect) to hit multiple targets.

Volibear

  • Defensive fighter, good alternate to Sion.
  • Great pushing tools, especially ultimate, which disables strucutres.
  • Can go mix of offense and defense or pure defense.
  • Similar to Sion; allows for aggressive playstyle.
  • Lightning looks cool.

Kennen

  • Magical lightning ninja.
  • Great tools for clear, scirmish, teamfights, and AOE burst.
  • Ultimate is a lightning storm, stuns and damages everyone around.
  • Expresses skills opposite Sion - shows flexibility.
  • Lightning looks cool.
  • Newest addition to the list.

Irelia

  • Plays her in unusual way as AP assassin.
  • Deadly, but questionable.
  • Considered a troll pick, but is capable of high damage.
  • Baus has suprised a lot of people with Irelia in some matches.
  • Not the most 'troll' off-meta pick, but not game breaking.

Jax

  • Here's to you, kid.
  • Great tools for splitpush, burst, and fighting, due to high attack speed.
  • Able to build either AP or AD.
  • Historically AD, Baus popularized full AP.
  • Similar to Irelia, this time more impressive.
  • Shows creativity and self-conviction of Baus.

Rammus

  • Great mobile defensive pick.
  • Unpopular in Baus's role, he found a good playstyle.
  • Deadly Armadillo.
  • Only says things like:
  • OK.
  • Alright.
  • Okay.
